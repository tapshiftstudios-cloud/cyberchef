## Full description

**Stop wasting food. Cook smarter.**

CyberChef is your AI-powered kitchen companion: scan what's in your fridge, read shopping receipts, track expiry dates, and get personalized recipes in seconds.

**SCAN YOUR KITCHEN**
• Fridge photo → ingredient list + 3 AI recipes
• Receipt scan → auto-add products to your pantry
• Barcode scan for quick product lookup
• Quick, Rescue, and Chef scan modes

**TRACK FRESHNESS**
• See what expires soon — critical, warning, safe
• Calendar view and unified pantry inventory
• Daily reminders so nothing goes to waste
• Savings dashboard: money and food saved

**SMART SHOPPING**
• Build your grocery list from scans
• Sync freshness data across sessions

**AI RECIPES**
• Recipes tailored to your diet and cuisine region
• 27 languages supported
• Save favorites and revisit recent scans

**FREE & PRO**
• Free: daily AI limits with optional rewarded scans
• Pro: higher limits, no ads, cloud sync

**PRIVACY**
Camera and photos are used only for scanning. See our privacy policy in Settings.

No login required for core features — continue as guest anytime.
